REDLINE {label}
The demo: the Undercity, from Wake to the Escape Tunnel. Placeholder art and sound. Keyboard or controller.
The full Act I continues in Lowlight.

RUN
  Windows: unzip, double-click REDLINE.exe. SmartScreen: "More info" -> "Run anyway" (unsigned).
  macOS:   unzip, right-click REDLINE.app -> Open (ad-hoc signed; or System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).
  Linux:   chmod +x REDLINE.x86_64 && ./REDLINE.x86_64
  Web:     open the page; the game starts after the first click.

TITLE SCREEN: New Game starts unarmed in the Undercity (Wake). Cutscenes: hold Space/Enter/K/Z (pad A) or
pause -> Skip scene. Settings has comfort and accessibility options; every one of them is in the demo.

CONTROLS (keyboard / pad)
  Move            A D / arrows          left stick / d-pad
  Jump            Space, K or Z         A
  Dodge           Shift, L or C         B
  Light attack    J                     X
  Heavy attack    I                     Y
  Ranged          O                     RB
  Heal            H
  Interact        E                     d-pad up
  Map             M                     Back
  Pause           Esc or P              Start   (in a browser, Esc leaves fullscreen: use P)
